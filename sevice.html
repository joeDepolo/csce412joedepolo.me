What is Camp Sweeney

Camp Sweeney is a camp for children with type 1 diabetes that are learning to live with and manage their diabetes. They accomplish through many outdoor activities, camper bonding, and thourough education on the disease.

My Project

As a LEGION volunteer, I go to the campus at least once a year to help maintain and refurbish the campsite. Also, I help raise money to offer scholarships for students who may not be able to afford the camp.

LEGION's Projects

LEGION is a social-service Men's Organization at Texas A&M University that aims to build young men into positive individuals that give back to the community.

We do many other projects in the Bryan-College Station area such as park cleanups, Aggie Build, H4H, and Big Event. We aim to give back to the community that has given so much to us.
