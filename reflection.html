Class Trip Reflection

During the Faculty-Led trip to France, our Cloud Computing class had the opportunity to visit CGM CMA in Marseille. CGM CMA is a company that is centered arounf the development of shipping solutions. While it started as a soley maritime company, it has since expanded to include a wide range of shipping solutions. This includes logistics, air transport, and other forms transportation. However, the company is not only focused on getting itmes from point A to point B, but also on the environmental impact of shipping as well as future R&D for their solutions. One of their biggest goals is they are trying to have a net 0 carbon footprint by 2050, and are working on solutions to help them achieve this goal. This accomplishment will help them create a cleaner environment by .11% of the worlds carbon emissions which is more than it seems. This includes using cleaner fuels and more efficient electricity consumption. The project we discussed the most in terms of computing is their SmartShip project. SmartShip is a project that uses AI to help optimize shipping routes and reduce fuel consumption. They also monitor the health and energy consumption of the ship and its crew. This is done through a combination of sensors and AI algorithms that analyze the data in real-time. This collects large sets of data which are then stored in the cloud and analyzed to help improve the efficiency and environmental health of the shipping process. This is then used to create a digital twin of the ship, which is a virtual representation of the ship that can be used to simulate different scenarios and optimize performance. Overall, the visit to CGM CMA was a great experience that allowed us to see how cloud computing is being used in a non Big Tech field. It was also interesting to learn how companies outside of the Unitied States are using cloud computing to solve real-world problems and improve their operations.
