Why Me

When applying to any job, it is important to ask the question “Why Me?”. Why am I the best person for this job? For me, the answer revolves around my ability to combine my computer science skills with my business aptitude to bridge the technical gap. Ever since I was young, I knew I was good at two things: Math and talking. I love solving complex equations just as much as I love socializing and growing my network. I found this to be very uncommon. I leaned into my interpersonal strengths by taking restaurant serving jobs in high school, and in the realm of math I began to gravitate towards coding when I started to take computer science courses. As I have translated my math skills into computer science skills, I have noticed even fewer people sharing the same interpersonal skills as I possess. I truly believe that bridging the gap between the technical coding side and the communication of the ideas is an essential part of the software development industry, and very few people have that skill like I do.

Over the summer of 2024 I was able to intern at Incyte Pharmaceuticals as a Data Engineer. I primarily created applications for creative utilizations of their data for various teams. After creating these products, I was clearly able to communicate them to various departments such as Sales, Medical Legal and Regulatory (MLR), and Market Access. This affirmed that I am able to not only develop some sort of software application but “sell it” too. But don’t just take my word for it, my manager and others on my team saw what I was doing and continued to ask for more. So when asking myself the question “Why Me?” I can look back on these experiences and answer that question. That is why any company should hire me.
